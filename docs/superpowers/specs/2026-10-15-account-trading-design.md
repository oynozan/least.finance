# Account trading: design

2026-10-15. The owner decided this in conversation, with a hackathon deadline about two hours away.

## Goal

Buy, Sell and Swap run from the account balance, like predictions and Long/Short. The person never signs anything, never sees a wallet popup, and never pays gas. We buy and hold real coins and record them as the person's holdings. A person can also send a held coin out to their own wallet. The wallet-signed swap flow is removed from the app.

## Decisions

| Topic                  | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fill                   | Instant, at the server's own catalog price (`server/src/swaps/catalog.ts`, LI.FI prices). The person trades against our inventory.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Price check            | The request carries the price the app showed. The server refuses with `price_moved` if its own price is more than 1% worse for the person. The server's price is always the one used.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Money                  | Buy: one Mongo transaction debits the balance (guarded `balance >= amount`), adds units to the holding, and writes a `trades` doc and a ledger entry (`trade_buy`, change −amount). Sell: subtracts units (guarded `units >= sold`), credits the balance (`trade_sell`, change +usd) and writes a `trades` doc. Swap: subtracts one holding and adds another, writes a `trades` doc and a `trade_swap` ledger entry with change 0. `requestId` is unique, so a repeat answers the first result. |
| Units                  | Holdings are Decimal128 coin units, rounded down to the coin's decimals on its home network. Dollars are USDC. The balance uses the ledger's existing wei units: 18 decimals on Arc.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Minimum                | $0.10 a trade.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Fees                   | None charged by us. The app shows "No fees, we cover network costs."                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Real coins (the hedge) | A `TradeHedger` worker nets unhedged trades per coin and buys or sells the net on the market through LI.FI. It moves money between the Arc treasury's USDC and our inventory wallet on the coin's home network, and records `hedges`. It is behind `TRADE_HEDGING`, off by default, until the inventory wallets are funded. Trades fill whether or not it runs.                                                                                                                                 |
| Inventory keys         | EVM uses one address on every EVM network, at `m/44'/60'/3'/0/0` of `DEPOSIT_MNEMONIC`, with nonces per chain. Solana uses `m/44'/501'/3'/0'`. These collide with no existing path: deposits and treasury use 0', perps 1', the vault 2'.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Gas                    | Ours. The inventory wallets keep native gas, and `trading-status` prints how much each holds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Coin withdrawals       | `holding:withdraw` subtracts the units in one transaction and writes a `coinWithdrawals` doc (`sending`). A `CoinSender` worker sends from the inventory wallet: a native transfer, or ERC-20 `transfer`, or a Solana system or SPL transfer. It records the transaction hash, marks the withdrawal `sent` once mined, and gives the units back on a definite failure. It is behind `COIN_WITHDRAWALS`, off by default; while off, requests are refused with `unavailable`.                     |
| App                    | Buy's cap is the account balance. Sell and Swap are capped by the holding. There is no network step: a coin is a coin. Review shows what you pay, what you get, "No fees, we cover network costs." and "Instant". Confirm is optimistic, like predictions: balance and holdings move at once and roll back on an error. The sidebar lists holdings (logo, units, live USD value), each with Send.                                                                                               |
| Removed                | The wallet-signed swap flow in the app: approve, sign, `swap:quote` and `swap:order` from the review. Server swap code stays dormant for now.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## Shared contract

`server/src/trading/types.ts` is the contract every part builds against. The app imports it from `@/server/src/trading/types`.

## Build split (parallel, 2026-10-15)

- **A, server core.** `server/src/trading/{trades.ts,holdings.ts,views.ts}`, new collections and ledger kinds in `mongo.ts`, zod in `validation.ts`, the `trade:order` and `holding:withdraw` socket handlers, holdings in the account snapshot, and pushes.
- **B, app.** Buy, Sell and Swap operations and the review from the balance, the account client and state reducer, sidebar holdings with Send, and removal of the wallet-signed path.
- **C, execution.** Inventory keys, `TradeHedger`, `CoinSender`, config switches, fakes and tests.
- **D, integration.** Wiring, the full checks, and a browser test of buy, sell, swap and send.
